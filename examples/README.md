# Anonymized Examples / 匿名示例

These are illustrative, anonymized scenarios. They contain no real customer identifiers.

## 1. Physician reports no intraoperative imaging use

**Context / 场景：** A surgical oncologist in Tegucigalpa has received the company/product introduction and says that their own procedures do not use intraoperative X-ray. The institution's equipment is still unknown.

**CRM summary / 登记摘要：**

> 洪都拉斯 Tegucigalpa 肿瘤外科医生。已介绍公司信息和产品信息，并询问其手术中 X 射线及术中透视设备的使用情况。对方明确表示不使用相关设备，当前 C 臂需求匹配度较低。下一步询问对方执业机构的设备使用情况。

## 2. Hospital staff identification and department referral

**Context / 场景：** A Cartagena hospital contact identified their institution. The seller asked for a contact in biomedical engineering or purchasing, with no answer yet.

**CRM summary / 登记摘要：**

> 哥伦比亚 Cartagena 医院工作人员。已介绍公司信息和产品信息，并询问医院医学影像设备情况及合作机会。对方确认所属机构，具体职务及设备配置情况尚未明确。已询问能否转介生物医学工程或采购部门负责人，目前等待回复。

## 3. Planned contact versus completed contact

**Not yet sent / 尚未发送：** “下一步询问医院是否有设备更新计划。”

**Already sent without a reply / 已发送待回复：** “已询问医院是否有设备更新计划，目前等待回复。”

Never convert a pending action into a completed one.
